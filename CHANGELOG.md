# Changelog

Security fixes are listed here as well as in each release's notes, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) promises.

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

The first public release.

- **The app:** Today, Roles with fit scores and checks, Profile with an AI interview, tailored resumes and cover letters with fact checks, outreach drafts, interview practice by typing or voice, Chat, Inbox (read-only Gmail), reminders and push notifications.
- **Every kind of search:** hourly, monthly or yearly pay in any common format and currency, commutes in miles or kilometers, licenses, certifications and projects on the resume (education first for early careers), roles scored without a posting, and schedule rules such as "no overnight shifts" in fit checks.
- **Your data:** Download my data gives spreadsheets of your records plus every document PDF; keys and tokens are left out.
- **Setup:** one line in Google Cloud Shell from a Windows PC, Mac or Chromebook. It builds a hardened server, walks through Google sign-in, signs in to Claude Code, and adds more people when run again.
- **Guides:** [start-here.md](docs/start-here.md), [devices.md](docs/devices.md), [install.md](docs/install.md), [user-guide.md](docs/user-guide.md), [cheap.md](docs/cheap.md), [security.md](docs/security.md) and [operations.md](docs/operations.md).
- **Security before release:** an AI-assisted security review (not an independent audit) found issues, now fixed, in the AI's handling of untrusted text, chat status changes, email sender checks, rate limits, spend tracking, link checks, push-button replay, sign-in state binding and backups. [security.md](docs/security.md) lists what's protected and the known limits.
