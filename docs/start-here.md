## 1. What Job Search HQ is

A private website for your job search, with an AI assistant built in. It runs on a small computer you rent from Google for little or nothing, so your information stays with you rather than with a job-search company. (The AI work is done by Anthropic's Claude, which receives what each task needs, such as your profile and a job posting.)

Each day it:
- **Collects jobs** from the LinkedIn and Indeed alert emails in your Gmail, and from company job pages you choose to watch.
- **Scores each job** against what you've told it about yourself, and checks your must-haves: location, pay, and kinds of employers you'd rather avoid.
- **Writes a tailored resume and cover letter** for any job you pick, using only your real experience, and checks them before you see them.
- **Drafts messages** to recruiters and people at the company, which you copy and send yourself.
- **Coaches interview answers**, typed or spoken, with a score and one thing to improve.
- **Keeps track** of what you applied to, who you're waiting on, and what's due, and reminds you on your phone or computer.

You stay in charge. It never applies for you, never sends email, never posts anything, and never adds a claim about you that you haven't approved.

## 2. Is it right for you?

**It works for every kind of job search.** First jobs and internships, hourly and shift work, returning after a break, changing careers, mid-career moves and senior roles all use the same tools. [user-guide.md, section 14](user-guide.md#14-making-it-work-for-your-search) has tips for each.

**You'll get the most from it if:**
- you're searching for more than a few weeks
- you find jobs through LinkedIn, Indeed or company career pages
- you apply to roles where a tailored resume and cover letter help

**Good to know before you start:**
- **It needs a Google account with Gmail.** That's where it reads job alerts and employer emails. It can only read: it can't send, delete or change anything. A free Gmail account works, and you can make a new one just for your search.
- **Google asks you to reconnect Gmail about once a week.** It's one tap, and the app reminds you. [Why](install.md#4-set-up-google-sign-in).
- **It's in English.** The setup's built-in time zone choices are for the US, though any time zone works. Pay can be in any currency, and resumes can use A4 paper.
- **Resumes can't yet show some names.** The resume's font has no Chinese, Japanese or Korean characters, or emoji, so a name written in them won't print. Names with accents, like Nguyễn or Zoë, work.
- **The AI can make mistakes.** Every document is checked against your profile, but read everything before you send it.
- **It isn't a job board.** It works with the jobs you're sent and the companies you choose.

## 3. Two jobs: the job seeker and the helper

There are two roles. One person can do both.

| | The job seeker | The helper |
|---|---|---|
| **Does** | Uses the website day to day | Sets up the server once, and adds people |
| **Needs** | A browser on any computer, tablet or phone | A computer with a keyboard and about 90 minutes |
| **Skills** | Using a website | Copying and pasting, following steps carefully |
| **Reads** | [user-guide.md](user-guide.md) | [install.md](install.md) and [devices.md](devices.md) |

**If you're not comfortable with computers,** ask a friend or family member to be your helper. They set up your copy once, from their own computer, and you only ever use the website. One helper can host copies for several people on one server, and each copy is private to its person.

**If you're doing it yourself,** you don't need to be technical. The setup asks questions one at a time with numbered choices, explains each answer, and asks again if something's wrong. You'll copy and paste a few things between two browser tabs. If you can fill in an online form, you can do this.

## 4. What you need

**Everyone:**
- [ ] **A personal Google account with Gmail.** Your own, or a new one for your search. School and work Google accounts usually don't work.
- [ ] **A browser.** Chrome is best on every device, and the setup needs Chrome (or Edge on Windows). For daily use, Edge and Safari work too. [devices.md](devices.md) covers each one.

**The helper also needs:**
- [ ] **A Windows PC, Mac or Chromebook** with a keyboard. Setup happens in a browser tab, so the steps are nearly the same on all three. A personal device is best: work and school computers often block the pages you'll need.
- [ ] **A credit or debit card** for the Google Cloud free trial. Google checks the card but doesn't charge it during the trial, and you can set an email alert if anything ever costs money.
- [ ] **A way to reach Claude, the AI.** Either:
  - **Claude Pro** ($20 a month at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai)), in the job seeker's own name. Each person needs their own plan; plans can't be shared.
  - **Or an Anthropic API key**, paid per use with a monthly limit you set. Light use often costs a few dollars a month.
- [ ] **About 90 minutes,** without needing to rush, including one wait of 10 to 25 minutes.
- [ ] **If you're setting it up for someone else,** get their Google email before you start. If they'll use a Claude plan, have them with you, or on the phone, for the last ten minutes, when they sign in to their own Claude account.
- [ ] **Optional, recommended for a long search:** your own domain name, about $12 a year from any domain seller. You can start without one and add it later. [install.md, step 7](install.md#7-your-own-domain-recommended).

## 5. What it costs

About **$20 a month**, which is mostly the Claude plan, plus about **$12 a year** for a domain if you buy one. The server is covered by Google's free trial for 90 days, then moves to Google's always-free server, where small charges, such as for its IP address, can add up to about $5 a month. [cheap.md](cheap.md) has the details and the best order to start each free trial.

Using a free LinkedIn Premium trial alongside it is optional.

## 6. Your path through the guides

**If you're the helper,** in this order:
1. **[cheap.md](cheap.md):** which free trials to start, and when (5 minutes).
2. **[devices.md, Part 1](devices.md#part-1-setting-up-from-your-computer):** the steps for your computer: opening the setup terminal, copying and pasting, and keeping two tabs side by side (10 minutes).
3. **[install.md](install.md):** the setup itself (about 90 minutes).
4. **Send the job seeker** their web address and [user-guide.md](user-guide.md).

**If you're the job seeker:**
1. **[devices.md, Part 2](devices.md#part-2-using-job-search-hq-on-each-device):** open your address, install it as an app, and turn on notifications on each device you use.
2. **[user-guide.md](user-guide.md):** your first hour, then each part of the app.

**If you're both,** follow the helper's path, then the job seeker's.

## 7. How long it takes

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Starting the Google Cloud trial and Claude | 10 to 15 minutes |
| Running the setup | A few minutes to make the server, then one wait of 10 to 25 minutes while the app builds |
| Google sign-in setup (four parts in the Google Cloud console) | 15 to 20 minutes the first time |
| The job seeker's first session: profile, Gmail, job alerts | About an hour, and it can be split up |
| Day to day | 10 to 20 minutes a morning, plus time on applications |

Nothing breaks if you stop partway. The setup can be run again, and it picks up where it left off.

## 8. Getting help

- **Each guide ends with a troubleshooting table:** [install.md](install.md#troubleshooting), [devices.md](devices.md#part-3-device-problems) and [user-guide.md](user-guide.md#13-if-something-goes-wrong).
- **Most messages on screen say what to do next.** Read the whole message before choosing anything.
- **If you're stuck during setup,** copy the last lines from the setup window into a message to whoever is helping you, or into a new issue on the project's GitHub page. Never include the Client secret, an API key or a password.
- **Security problems:** report them privately, as [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) explains.

## Words you will see

| Word | What it means here |
|---|---|
| **Server** | A computer that's always on, in a Google data center, that runs your Job Search HQ. You rent it through Google Cloud. |
| **Google Cloud** | Google's service for renting servers. It's separate from Gmail, but you use the same Google account. |
| **Free trial** | Google Cloud gives new accounts $300 of credit for 90 days. Job Search HQ uses a small part of it. |
| **Always-free server** | A small server Google lets each account run at no charge. Job Search HQ moves there before the trial ends. |
| **Project** | A folder in Google Cloud that holds everything for Job Search HQ. The setup makes one for you. |
| **Billing account** | Where Google keeps your card and trial credit. |
| **Cloud Shell** | A text window in your browser, run by Google, where you type or paste commands. It's where the setup runs, so you don't install anything on your computer. |
| **Terminal** or **command** | A window where you type instructions instead of clicking. You'll mostly paste one line and answer questions. |
| **SSH** and **sudo** | SSH connects Cloud Shell to your server; `sudo` runs a command on the server as its administrator. You only need them for occasional maintenance. |
| **IAP tunnel** | Google's secure way in to your server for SSH. It's the only way in besides the website, and it only works for your Google account. |
| **IP address** | The number that identifies your server on the internet, like `203.0.113.10`. The setup reserves one that stays the same. |
| **Docker** | The software that runs the app on your server, with each person's copy in its own sealed-off box (a container). The setup installs it. |
| **Snapshot** | A saved copy of a server's disk. The move to the free server keeps one, so you can go back. |
| **Installer** or **setup** | The script that builds your server. It asks questions with numbered answers. |
| **Region** and **zone** | Which Google data center holds your server, like `us-east1` (South Carolina). Pick the one nearest you; us-east1 is a fine choice if unsure. The zone is a part of it, like `us-east1-b`. |
| **e2-medium** and **e2-micro** | The two server sizes: the comfortable trial size, and Google's smaller always-free size. |
| **Project ID** | Your project's name, like `jobhq-261104-123`. It's in the project picker at the top of the Google Cloud console. |
| **Web address** | Where your copy lives, like `pat.jobs.example.com`. Each person has their own. |
| **Domain** | A name you buy, like `example.com`, about $12 a year. Optional but recommended. |
| **DNS record** | A setting at your domain seller that points your address at your server. The setup tells you exactly what to enter. |
| **A record** | The kind of DNS record the setup asks for. It points a name at your server's IP address. |
| **sslip.io** | A free outside service that gives you a working web address with no domain. Fine for trying it, at your own risk. |
| **HTTPS** | The padlock in the address bar. Every address gets one automatically. |
| **Google sign-in client** | The setting in Google Cloud that puts a "Sign in with Google" button on your copy and lets it read Gmail. |
| **Client ID** and **Client secret** | Two codes Google gives you for the sign-in client. You paste them into the setup. Keep the secret private, like a password. |
| **Google Auth Platform** | The part of the Google Cloud console where you set up sign-in. Its pages are Overview, Branding, Audience, Data Access (scopes) and Clients. |
| **Scopes** | What the app asks Google permission for: your email address, and reading Gmail. |
| **Redirect URI** | The address Google sends you back to after you sign in, like `https://pat.example.com/auth/callback`. The setup prints it. |
| **Test user** | A Google account you've allowed to connect its Gmail. Each job seeker must be on the list. |
| **Claude** | The AI, made by Anthropic, that does the writing, scoring and coaching. |
| **Claude Code** | Anthropic's own program, which runs Claude on your server. Signing in to it is how a Claude Pro plan connects. |
| **API key** | A long password-like code from Anthropic for paying per use instead of with a plan. |
| **Install as an app** | Saving the website so it opens in its own window, with its own icon. Nothing is downloaded from an app store. |
| **Notification** | A pop-up on your phone or computer, for reminders and news. |
| **Profile** | Everything you've told Job Search HQ about your work, skills and goals. Every resume line comes from it. |
| **Applicant tracking system** | The software employers use to collect and scan applications, such as Greenhouse or Workday. Job Search HQ checks that it can read your resume. |
| **Job board** | A page that lists a company's open jobs. Many companies use Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby for theirs, and Job Search HQ can watch those. |
| **CSV** | A simple spreadsheet file that Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers can open. **Download my data** gives you these. |
